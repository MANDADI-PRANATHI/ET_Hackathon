INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT — Ref INC-2026-014 — 2026-05-18

On 2026-05-18 crude feed pump P-101A tripped on high vibration with a failed
mechanical seal. Standby pump p-101b was started. Pressure safety valve PSV-110A
on reflux drum V-204 lifted and reseated within the limits of OISD-STD-105.

The overdue statutory inspection on PSV110B was noted as a contributing gap.
Vibration had been trending up for 3 weeks (a rise of about 40%) before the trip
on 12/05/2026.
